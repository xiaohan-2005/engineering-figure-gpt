# Example: Mathematical Modeling Framework

## Figure Brief

- **Figure goal:** explain a complete modeling workflow in one publication-style framework figure.
- **Paper claim:** the solution combines preprocessing, prediction, optimization, and robustness analysis in a staged pipeline.
- **Figure type:** `mathematical-model-framework`
- **Mode:** `image`
- **Panels/modules:** problem & data; preprocessing; forecasting model; optimization model; model validation; sensitivity analysis; decision output.
- **Must-keep labels:** Forecasting, Optimization, Sensitivity Analysis.
- **Data:** not applicable for the conceptual framework panel.
- **Style constraints:** white background, landscape layout, concise Chinese labels, clear arrows, competition-paper readability.
- **Verification:** no invented formulas or values; dependencies between submodels are explicit; labels remain readable at paper width.
