# Publication Figure Design

Default design rules:

- white or near-white background
- short, paper-readable labels
- explicit reading order and directional arrows
- balanced spacing and modular alignment
- restrained, low-saturation semantic color use
- avoid decorative 3D effects and unnecessary gradients

For mathematical-modeling figures, show the reasoning chain from problem/data through assumptions, preprocessing, model construction, solution/optimization, validation, sensitivity/robustness analysis, and final output. Preserve supplied symbols, variables, units, and model names.

For exact plots, preserve the supplied scale and values. Do not change numbers to improve appearance.
